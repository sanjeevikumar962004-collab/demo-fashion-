The Salem Sartorial Standard

Every garment commissioned at stackly. undergoes triple fitting tolerances. Before cutting into raw Salem Mulberry Silk or virgin Italian Cashmere, a muslin toile prototype is constructed to guarantee drape accuracy.

1

Muslin Fitting: We drape an unbleached cotton prototype to test silhouette posture.

2

Baste Stage: Hand-basted stitching allows adjustments along armhole and hemline.

3

Final Hand Press: Hand-ironed with traditional steam presses in Salem.
