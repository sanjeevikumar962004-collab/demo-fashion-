Common Questions

BEFORE
YOU
VISIT

Everything you need to know about visiting the stackly. atelier in Salem.

Do I need an appointment to visit the atelier?

+

Walk-ins are welcome during regular atelier hours (Monday–Saturday). However, for a private one-on-one session with a dedicated stylist and access to the full bespoke catalogue, we strongly recommend booking in advance. Sunday visits are strictly by appointment.

Is the private consultation complimentary?

+

Yes, absolutely. All private consultations at the stackly. atelier are complimentary and carry no purchase obligation. We believe every client relationship should begin with trust and transparency.

How long does a bespoke order take?

+

A typical bespoke commission requires 6–8 weeks from initial consultation to final delivery, with typically 3 fittings. For complex multi-piece orders or special occasion wear, allow 10–12 weeks. We also offer an expedited 3-week service with a premium.

Do you ship ready-to-wear anywhere in India?

+

Yes, we ship all ready-to-wear pieces across India via fully insured, white-glove courier. International shipping to select destinations is available upon request. All garments are packaged in our signature stackly. archival boxes.

Can I see the current collection online before visiting?

+

Absolutely. Our full Autumn/Winter 2026 collection is available to browse on our Products page. You can add items to your wishlist and bring that list to your consultation — our team will have your selections ready to try.
